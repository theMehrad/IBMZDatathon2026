/sections/4/category/0/data/10/title
Mini Challenge - Week 1

/sections/4/category/0/data/10/description
What Is the Mini Challenge Series? This Series is a 6-week practice run leading into IBM Z Datathon 2026 — it is NOT the Datathon itself. Each week is a smaller, standalone AI-for-Good challenge designed to build your team's technical skills and confidence before the main event. 👉 Completing mini challenge weeks does not itself decide the Datathon winners — it's preparation, mentorship and momentum so your team walks into the Datathon ready to build, integrate and pitch with confidence. Why the Mini Challenges Matter Each week is scoped to build specific skills, so by Week 6 your team has touched those skill areas which will help you for the final Datathon challenge: • Week 1- Dataset, understanding problem statement, Data analytics and turning raw data into insight • Week 2- Python, data ingestion, prompt writing, AI fundamentals • Week 3- GenAI output quality, accessibility and responsible-AI checks, Trust, Identity and security • Week 4- embeddings, similarity search and explainable matching logic (Mentor Support) • Week 5- anomaly detection and generating explainable, decision-ready alerts (Mentor Support) • Week 6- full end-to-end prototyping, architecture and a live pitch (Video pitch) (Mentor Support) Mini Challenges’ - Schedule Week-1 Friday, 4 Sep 2026 - Sun, 6 Sep 2026 (Duration 48 Hours) Week-2 Friday, 11 Sep 2026 - Sun, 13 Sep 2026 (Duration 48 Hours) Week-3 Friday, 18 Sep 2026 - Sun, 20 Sep 2026 (Duration 48 Hours) Week-4 Friday, 25 Sep 2026 - Sat, 26 Sep 2026 (Duration 24 Hours) Week-5 Friday, 2 Oct 2026 - Sat, 3 Oct 2026 (Duration 24 Hours) Week-6 Friday, 9 Oct 2026 - Sat, 10 Oct 2026 (Duration 24 Hours) 🏆How Weekly Winners Are Identified • Each week's submissions are reviewed the following weekend by the SSF team against that week's scoring rubric — Attempting Mini challenge within timeframe, problem understanding & analysis, technical implementation, AI/GenAI quality, reliability & responsible AI, innovation & UX, security implication and documentation. • A shortlist and leaderboard update are published before the next week's challenge launches. • Recognized participants get a leaderboard feature and visibility heading into Datathon participation— mini challenges' performance is a readiness signal, not a Datathon score. ✅ Before the Final Datathon The Mini Hackathon Series wraps a full week before IBM Z Datathon 2026 (17–18 Oct). Use that final week to rest, absorb mentor feedback, and lock in your Datathon strategy — you'll walk in ready.

FAQs

/sections/7/category/0/title


/sections/7/category/0/data/0/title
What is the IBM Z Datathon 2026?

/sections/7/category/0/data/0/description
The IBM Z Datathon 2026 is a global, 24-hours event organised by Shooting Stars Foundation (SSF) in partnership with IBM. It brings together college students from around the world to build innovative AI-powered solutions using IBM Z and LinuxONE infrastructure. Participants work in teams to solve real-world challenges across three themes: Real-Time AI for Critical Decisions, AI Secured: Innovation Without Exposure, and a Wildcard category for bold and original ideas — all under the overarching "AI for Good" mission. The event runs across 8 in-person university hubs globally (India, South Africa, UK, Malaysia, Australia, USA) plus a fully open virtual track, making it accessible to students everywhere.

/sections/7/category/0/data/1/title
Who can participate in the IBM Z Datathon?

/sections/7/category/0/data/1/description
The IBM Z Datathon is open exclusively to college and university students worldwide — undergraduate and postgraduate. You do not need prior experience with IBM Z or mainframe technologies to participate. Students from any discipline are welcome — whether you come from computer science, data science, business, design, or any other field. Diverse, cross-functional teams are highly encouraged. The candidate should be a currently enrolled student. Students from all countries may participate in the online (virtual) track. In-person participation is tied to your nearest registered hub college.

/sections/7/category/0/data/2/title
How do I register for the Datathon?

/sections/7/category/0/data/2/description
Registration is free. Visit the official event page at starhacks.org/ibm-z-datathon-2026 and click the Register button. You will be prompted to select your participation track — Virtual (global, open to all) or one of the 8 In-Person hubs (Bangalore, Chennai, London, Malaysia, Australia, South Africa, Dallas, or New York). Individual registration is required first. Once registered, your team lead must complete the Team Registration Form on H2S platform. If you do not yet have a team, check the team formation section under Timelines. Registration deadline: October 1, 2026 [midnight]. After this date, registrations will be placed on a waitlist.

/sections/7/category/0/data/3/title
Do I register as an individual or as a team?

/sections/7/category/0/data/3/description
Both steps are required. Every participant must first register individually through the main registration form on starhacks.org. The recommended team size is 4 to 6 members. Once your team is formed, the designated team lead must complete a separate Team Registration Form, listing all members. If you do not have teammates yet, explore the Team formation section after individual registration on the H2S platform.

/sections/7/category/0/data/4/title
What are the Datathon themes for 2026?

/sections/7/category/0/data/4/description
There are three challenge themes for IBM Z Datathon 2026: 1. Real-Time AI for Critical Decisions (Detect. Decide. Respond.) — Build AI systems that analyse streaming data, identify risk, and support high-stakes decisions as events unfold. 2. AI Secured: Innovation Without Exposure (Powerful AI. Protected Data.) — Create privacy-preserving AI that learns from sensitive datasets while protecting identity, trust, and compliance. 3. Wildcard: Beyond the Obvious (Your Boldest AI + Data Idea.) — Push boundaries with a novel, data-driven AI solution that is responsible, technically ambitious, and wildly innovative. All themes fall under the overarching mission of AI for Good. Your solution must demonstrate why IBM Z / LinuxONE is the right platform for your idea.

/sections/7/category/0/data/5/title
What technology and infrastructure will be provided?

/sections/7/category/0/data/5/description
Registered participants will receive access to IBM Z / LinuxONE computing infrastructure at no cost. This is a major advantage — you get to work on the same enterprise-grade platform that powers the world's most critical industries. IBM Z Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) will be available as mentors throughout the datathon window via the Discord helpdesk to support you with technical queries. You must arrange your own laptop, stable internet/WiFi, and basic development tools. Open-source datasets, public APIs, and standard libraries are permitted, provided their use is legal and properly licensed. A mandatory "Get Your LinuxONE System Ready" technical setup session will be held on October 16, 2026 (the day before the Datathon). Attendance is compulsory for all participating teams.

/sections/7/category/0/data/6/title
What is the "Get Your LinuxONE System Ready" session and is it mandatory?

/sections/7/category/0/data/6/description
Yes — this session is mandatory for all registered participants. It is scheduled for October 16, 2026, and will run across all time zones simultaneously: The session helps you set up access credentials, test your connection to the IBM LinuxONE system, troubleshoot any environment issues, and understand the submission platform.
